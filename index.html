Your complete guide to building with Nexus Prime

Get your first AI-powered app running in under 60 seconds.

1

Describe what you want to build

Type a clear goal into the Mission bar. Be specific: "Build a personal finance tracker with charts and a budget planner" works far better than "Make an app."

2

Click DEPLOY and watch the agents work

The system activates 5 AI agents simultaneously — Orchestrator, HTML, Data, JS, CSS — and a QA Critic that grades and polishes the output.

3

Launch your app from Agent Library

When synthesis is complete, your app appears in the Agent Library tab. Click Launch to open it in a new tab — it runs live, right now.

4

Your library is private to you

Every device gets its own unique ID. Your agents are 100% private — no other user can see or access them.

Best prompts include: the type of app, key features, the audience, and any design preference (e.g., "dark mode, minimalist").

Understand what types of apps you can build and how to manage them.

Dashboard

Analytics panels, data monitors, KPI trackers. Great for business metrics and real-time data visualization.

e.g. "Sales performance dashboard with weekly charts"

Chatbot

Conversational AI assistants for customer support, FAQ bots, or learning companions.

e.g. "Customer support bot for a shoe store"

Tool

Utility apps like calculators, converters, generators, or form builders.

e.g. "Invoice generator with PDF export"

Landing Page

Marketing pages, product showcases, and portfolio sites with premium design.

e.g. "Landing page for a fitness coaching app"

Tricks used by advanced users to get the best results.

01
Be specific with data — Instead of "a finance app", say "a budget tracker with income/expense categories, pie charts, and a monthly savings goal of $500."
02
Mention design style — "Dark mode, glassmorphism, purple accent" gives the CSS Agent clear direction and produces stunning results.
03
Name your audience — "A dashboard for gym owners tracking 50+ members" helps all agents understand the scale and complexity.
04
Watch the Pipeline tab — Open the Pipeline view before clicking Deploy to watch all 5 agents activate and process in real-time.
05
Build a suite — Create multiple apps for the same business (Admin Dashboard + Customer Chatbot + Report Tool) and link them together to build a full product.
