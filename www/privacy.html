Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

1. Introduction

AON AI Nexus Prime ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle technical metadata and AI interactions within the platform.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect personal identifyable information (PII) unless explicitly provided by you (e.g., API keys). Your Gemini and GitHub tokens are stored locally on your device or in your private environment variables and are never transmitted to our servers.

3. AI Interactions

Interactions with the AI synthesis engine are processed through the Google Generative AI and GitHub Models APIs. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for how they handle prompt data.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AI SDK
  • GitHub Models API
  • Render (Hosting)

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@agentmaximize.com.
