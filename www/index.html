Operational Manual

Nexus Prime v5.0 Platform Architecture

MISSION CONTROL

Type your goal in the center bar. The "Orchestrator" analyzes your intent and defines a Blueprint which includes the UI theme, logic requirements, and data architecture.

MULTI-AGENT SYNTHESIS

Our parallel factory triggers 5 agents: HTML, Data, JS, CSS, and the QA Critic. They work simultaneously to build, refine, and verify your app in seconds.

SELF-HEALING

Every generated app is protected. If a logic error occurs, the Aon Neural Surgeon automatically patches the code to restore functionality without manual debugging.

AGENT LIBRARY

Store, audit, and launch your fleet of AI agents. Each agent is a standalone production-ready application hosted on your Nexus server.
