Request Data Deletion

At Prompt Maximize, we value your privacy. Since we use anonymous device identifiers instead of user accounts, you can request the deletion of your device-level data at any time.

Steps to delete your data:

  1. Open the Prompt Maximize app.
  2. Go to the Sidebar.
  3. Locate your Device ID at the bottom of the footer.
  4. Email us with your Device ID to the address below.

Submit your deletion request to:

Raj.bans@ymail.com

Once received, your device identifier and all associated trial/subscription history will be permanently wiped from our secure database within 48-72 hours.
