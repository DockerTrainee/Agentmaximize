Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

1. Data Collection

Prompt Maximize ("we", "our") takes your privacy seriously. We collect minimal data required to provide our prompt synthesis services. This includes:

  • Device Identifiers: We use anonymized device IDs to manage your 7-day trial and subscription status.
  • Input Content: The text you input for refinement is processed by our AI models (powered by Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude) to generate your optimized prompts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for:

  • Providing and improving our prompt synthesis services.
  • Preventing fraud and enforcing our 7-day free trial policy.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

3. AI Safety & Reporting

In compliance with Google Play Store policies, we provide mechanisms to report offensive content. Reported content is used only to improve our safety filters and model instructions.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize the following subprocessors for AI processing:

  • Google (Gemini AI Models)
  • Anthropic (Claude AI Models)
  • Render (Hosting & Infrastructure)

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page.
